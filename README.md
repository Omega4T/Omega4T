> **Building decentralized applications that are secure, efficient, and usable in the real world.**

I am a software engineering graduate from Indonesia, specializing in Web3 and blockchain development. My work sits between smart contracts and the interface people actually use: I care about getting the on-chain logic right, and about making sure the experience on top of it holds up.

<br/>

## ✿ Engineering Principles

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <b>🔐 Secure by default</b><br/>
      <sub>Contracts handle value. I treat security as a design constraint from the first line, not a review step at the end.</sub>
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <b>⚡ Efficient by design</b><br/>
      <sub>Clean architecture and gas-conscious implementation, on-chain and in the frontend that talks to it.</sub>
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <b>🌸 Usable by people</b><br/>
      <sub>A dApp nobody can use is a whitepaper. I build interfaces that make the underlying technology approachable.</sub>
    </td>
  </tr>
